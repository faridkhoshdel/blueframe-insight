# User guide - blueFrame Insight

## Login

1. Open https://blueframe-frontend.onrender.com
2. Email: admin@blueframe.com
3. Password: admin123456

## Pages

- /executive: manager dashboard (KPIs, trends, alerts)
- /crm: customers and deals
- /inventory: products and stock
- /sales: sales reports
- /analytics: data analysis
- /sentiment: feedback sentiment analysis
- /agents: AI agents
- /graph: customer network
- /simulator: scenario simulator
- /ai-insights: AI insights
- /multimodal: voice, image, text

## Inventory (warehouse staff)

- Add product: Inventory > New product > name, SKU, category, price, stock, min stock > Save
- Stock in or out: Inventory > Products > select > Movement > type IN or OUT > quantity > confirm
- Alerts: Inventory > Low-Stock Alerts

## CRM (sales team)

- Add customer: CRM > Customers > New > name, email, phone, company, status > Save
- Log activity: click customer > Add activity > type Call or Meeting or Email, date, notes
- Create deal: CRM > Deals > New > title, customer, amount, stage > Save
- AI scoring: click deal > AI Lead Score (0-100); click customer > AI Churn Risk

## AI agents

- Agents > Run All Agents: retention, nurture, cross-sell, follow-up
- Results: Agents > Logs

## Sentiment

- Sentiment > paste feedback text > Analyze > result: satisfied, angry, or neutral

## Simulator

- Price Change: product + percent > sales impact forecast
- Marketing Campaign: budget + type > ROI forecast
- Customer Loss: select customer > revenue impact

## Tips by role

- Sales managers: check executive dashboard daily; run churn risk weekly
- Visitors: check churn risk before visit; log activity after visit
- Warehouse: check low-stock daily; record movements immediately
- Logistics: prioritize shipments; update statuses; report delays

## Troubleshooting

- Page not loading: clear cache or use incognito; wait 1 min (cold start)
- API error: refresh; contact admin
- Forgot password: contact admin
